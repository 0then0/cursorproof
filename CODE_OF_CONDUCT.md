# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in CursorProof issues, pull requests and other project
spaces is expected to treat others with respect, regardless of background,
identity or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical choices with evidence and constructive feedback.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different levels of experience and reasonable disagreements.
- Respect privacy and stop unwanted contact when asked.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption and publishing private information without permission
are unacceptable. These rules also apply when someone represents the project
outside its own spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report misconduct privately to the maintainer at
[zheltov_pe@mail.ru](mailto:zheltov_pe@mail.ru). Include links, relevant context
and how you can be contacted. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.
If the maintainer is involved in the incident, use
[GitHub's reporting channels](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer will assess reports in context and limit disclosure of private
details to what is needed to address the incident. Depending on severity and
repetition, responses may include a warning, removal of content, a temporary
restriction or a permanent ban from project spaces. Enforcement should be
proportionate and explain the behavior that needs to change.
